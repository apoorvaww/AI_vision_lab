COMPLETE WORKFLOW

A Guided Journey From Raw Pixels to Empirical Insights

Every step of the computer vision lifecycle is automated, transparent, and reproducible.

01

Dataset Upload & Auto-Detection

Drag-and-drop or select dataset ZIP files. VisionLab automatically discovers class structures, handles single wrappers, flags corrupt images, and verifies split layouts.

02

AI Preprocessing Recommendations

Data-driven recommendations for resizing, ImageNet normalization, augmentation, and class balancing based on measured sample size, resolution skew, and class balance.

03

Diverse Deep Learning Models

Train Small CNN, Deep CNN, ResNet18, VGG16, EfficientNet-B0, MobileNetV2, or classical ML baselines (Random Forest, XGBoost, SVM) on identical holdout splits.

04

Live Training Progress

Monitor real-time training loss, validation accuracy, epoch progression, and detailed PyTorch execution logs with non-blocking background workers.

05

Empirical Holdout Evaluation

Genuine evaluation metrics on unseen holdouts: Multiclass Accuracy, Precision, Recall, F1-Score, ROC-AUC, styled Confusion Matrix, and epoch loss curves.

06

Model Comparison & Export

Compare multiple trained architectures side-by-side on identical benchmarks. Export downloadable PDF reports, CSV metrics, and reproducible Jupyter Notebooks.
